JBS HQ — Privacy Policy

JBS HQ is the private business app of Jack's Beef Shack (Bold Business Ventures LLC), Brooklyn, NY. It is used only by the owner and staff of Jack's Beef Shack. It is not for the public.

What the app stores

Orders, customers (names, phone numbers, addresses), payments, products, stock, expenses, and staff hours that the business enters. This information is kept in the business's own Google account (Google Sheets / Google Apps Script). A copy of recent screens is kept on the device so the app opens quickly and works offline.

How it is used

Only to run the business: make and deliver orders, send invoices and order texts, and pay staff. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. There are no ads and no third-party tracking.

Services used

Google (data storage), Twilio (text messages), Square (card payments) — only for the purposes above.

Sign-in

Staff sign in with a name and PIN given by the owner. Logging out clears the saved copy on the device.

Questions or deleting information

Email jacksbeefshack@gmail.com and we will answer or delete your information.
